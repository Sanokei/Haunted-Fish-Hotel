LIFECYCLE
Before approving activation, ValidateAuthoredBindings(out error) must pass.
On scene entry call StartAmbient once. Play requests an immediate white-frame strike.
StopAmbient cancels motion/clock and restores authored light intensity/color/enabled,
window visibility, projection property blocks, Canvas and shadow sprite/flip/pose/
scale/tint. Stop/disable/destroy are idempotent; events notify one start/stop per lifetime.
Disable/unload audio root or call StopAudio for immediate cancellation of all sources
and queued DSP cues; it unsubscribes light/dialogue events. Start fresh in next scene.
Do not keep both departing and arriving storms active through scene transitions.
